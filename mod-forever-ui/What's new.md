# What's new in ForeverUI

ForeverUI brings the modern World of Warcraft interface (the "camelot" client)
to a 3.3.5a client. This page lists everything it does. Every text follows the
client's language: English and French are provided.

## Customize UI

- **Esc > Customize UI**, or `/fui`, opens the editor: a grid over the screen,
  and every element of the interface under a blue veil with its name.
- Grid spacing from 20 to 300, and a 3 × 3 selector for the grid origin (a
  corner, an edge or the middle), whose lines stand out.
- Drag an element to move it. Click it to select it: nine handles appear on its
  box, and the handle held is the reference while dragging.
- **Snap on Grid**: an element is attracted by the grid lines near it.
  **Sticky UI**: it is attracted by the edges of the elements near it.
- A window beside the selected element sets its size, from 50 to 200 %.
- **Validate** saves, **Cancel** (or Esc) restores the layout found on opening,
  **Reset** puts everything back to default after a confirmation. Entering
  combat cancels the session.
- Movable elements: player, target, party and raid frames, cast bar, action
  bar, each gryphon, stance bar, pet bar, micro menu, bags bar, experience and
  reputation bars, buffs, debuffs, minimap and quest tracker.
- `/fui reset` puts every element back to default outside the editor.

## Unit frames

- **Player frame**: portrait, health and power bars, rest and combat states,
  threat, vehicle art, PvP icon, raid group, play time, incoming damage, and the
  death knight runes.
- **Target and target of target**: the art of each classification (normal,
  minus, rare, elite, rare elite, world boss), the reputation band and the
  reaction colors.
- **Pet frame.**
- **Party frames** beside the screen edge, and **compact raid frames** in
  groups; both work in combat.
- **Cast bar**: casts, channels, uninterruptible casts with the shield, and the
  interrupted state.

## Bars

- **Action bar**: the client's buttons in the modern style with all their
  states, the page number block, the two gryphons. The button art reaches every
  action button, the secondary bars included, and the stance and form bar
  slides in as in the client.
- **Stance bar** (forms, auras, aspects) and **pet bar**, on the small modern
  button.
- **Micro menu**: the modern buttons, with a professions button and the guild
  tabard on the Social button; the PvP and Help buttons are gone (Help Request
  moves to the Esc menu). The latency shows as a thin line.
- **Bags bar**: backpack, bag slots and keyring.
- **Experience and reputation bars**, the reputation colored by standing.

## Around the screen

- **Minimap** in its modern frame: clock, calendar, player coordinates, and the
  arrows at its edge resized for the larger map.
- **Buffs and debuffs** with their timers, weapon enchants and consolidated
  buffs.
- **Chat**: the modern layout, a button column, a thin scroll bar with a
  flashing return-to-bottom button, the friends button, tabs, dock and input
  box. At rest only the messages show; the window lights up under the mouse.
  Holding Alt and dragging selects text, Ctrl+C copies it.
- **Tooltips** in the modern frame, in the colors each tooltip asks for (other
  addons' included), with the comparison header and the red cross of a linked
  item.
- **Quest tracker**: quests and achievements under folding headers, objectives
  ticked as they are met, a sort and filter menu, a left click opening the quest
  on the map.
- **Menus and dialogs**: every dropdown and right-click menu, and the
  confirmation boxes, in the modern style.
- Scroll bars hide when there is nothing to scroll, and the content takes the
  room.

## Character sheet

- The modern window: model, equipment slots, level and class, resistances, and
  the stats on a banded background under category headers. The right pane
  folds away; the side tabs stay in place.
- Right pane: **character stats**, the **equipment manager** (set cards in the
  player's order, worn set checked, rename, delete, New Set, icon picker) and
  the **titles** earned, the worn one ticked.
- **Reputation**: folding blocks, a standing bar per faction, the faction's
  description with its switches, factions at war in their own color.
- **Skills**: folding blocks, a bar per skill and its description.
- **PvP**: season, rank badge and the ring filling towards the next rank, honor
  statistics, arena teams with a detail window, and the battlegrounds with their
  rewards and join buttons.
- **Currency**: every currency by category, with its icon and the client's two
  switches.
- **Pet**: the pet in 3D, its level, general statistics and resistances.
- **Statistics**: the modern Statistics tab, with folding categories, and 18
  statistics the camelot client has and 3.3.5 lacks: boss kills in classic
  dungeons, raids entered by size, deaths in 20 and 40 player raids.
- Large windows (character sheet, spellbook, talents) never overlap each other
  wrongly: the one opened or clicked comes to the front.

## Spellbook, talents and professions

- **Spellbook** on one page or two: a category per tab, spells in a grid,
  passives round, similar spells grouped on flyouts, a settings menu (hide
  passives, show all ranks), and a search with its preview and the "Missing from
  action bar" filter. Everything works in combat.
- **Talents**: the three trees with their nodes, arrows and gates, unspent
  points, specialization and pet side tabs, glyphs, a preview of pending
  choices, and a search with its options, preview and node markers.
- **Professions**: the professions book opened by its micro button, and the
  crafting window with its list, schematic, rank bar and side tabs.

## World map and quest log

- The map in a movable window, windowed or maximized, with the floor selector of
  dungeons and dungeon or raid maps by name.
- The quest log as a pane beside the map: the list, then each quest's page.
- The mouse wheel zooms towards the cursor, and a drag moves the zoomed map.

## Bags, bank and trade

- **Bags**: the modern panel with a search (non-matching items veiled), a sort
  button, the round portrait, and under the money the currencies the player
  watches.
- **Bank**: the bank and its bags on one grid, with the same search and sort.
- **Guild bank**, **merchant** (repairs, buyback, items in their quality color),
  **trade window**, **mailbox** (inbox, sending, open letter) and **auction
  house** (its three tabs in the modern frame).

## Social and groups

- **Social window**: contacts (friends and ignored), who, guild (roster, member
  detail, guild information, event log), chat channels and raid, with bottom
  tabs.
- **Guild tabard** window, the character wearing the design.
- **Group finder**: the dungeon finder and the raid browser in one movable
  window with side tabs; roles, dungeon types with their description and
  rewards, the specific dungeons list, listing a raid group and browsing raids.

## NPC windows

- Gossip and quest dialogs, **trainers** (categories, description in the
  tooltip), **flight maps** (the modern maps of Eastern Kingdoms and Kalimdor),
  **stable**, **gem socketing**.
- **Barber shop** in the modern full-screen layout; with the `Wow.exe` patch,
  choices are numbered, show their color swatch and open as a list of names.

## Menus and settings

- **Esc menu** with the modern red buttons, Help Request, and Customize UI.
- **Video, sound and interface options**, **key bindings**, **macros** and their
  icon picker, **chat settings**, **color picker** and opacity slider.
- **Clock window** (with an alarm check box), **zone map** (Shift+M), **dressing
  room**, and the **inspection** of another player: equipment, PvP rank, and
  talents in a window of their own.
- The achievements window can be moved; the tutorial is hidden.

## Login screens

- Login screen, main menu, **character selection** (character cards, rotation
  buttons), dialogs, **realm list**, **AddOns list**, **options** (video and
  sound), **cinematics** and **credits**, in the modern style.
- **Character creation** in two steps: race, class and body, then appearance
  and name, with camera buttons. With the `Wow.exe` patch, appearance choices
  are numbered, show their color swatch (skin tinted by the scene's light) and
  open as a list.
- The scenery models are scaled down so the characters keep their size on the
  wider modern scene.

## Server and optional parts

- **Installer**: `installer.exe` puts the addon, the art, the login screens and
  the statistics in place, and removes them all when run again (see
  `README.md`).
- **SQL file** (`sql/foreverui_statistics.sql`): lets the server count the boss
  kills of the added statistics.
- **`Wow.exe` patch** (`tools/patcher/ForeverUIPatcher.exe`): numbered
  appearance choices at character creation and the barber; Restore gives the
  original file back.
- With [mod-pvp-titles-ext](https://github.com/Adrestie/mod-pvp-titles-ext) on
  the server, the PvP tab shows the rank thresholds and the Dishonored state,
  with its debuff.

## For addon authors

- `ForeverUI.AddMicroButton` adds a button to the micro menu, and
  `ForeverUI.UpdateMicro` shows it pressed while its window is open.
- A language is one texts file per locale, added after the English one.

## Kept as in the client

The calendar, the help window, the arena registrar, the look of the
achievements window, and the login screens not listed above.
