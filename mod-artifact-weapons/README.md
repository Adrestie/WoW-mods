# mod-artifact-weapons

An AzerothCore module (WotLK 3.3.5a) that adds artifact weapons, each bound to
one class and carrying effects of its own. A weapon is earned through a book:
the books drop in Icecrown Citadel, and each one starts a quest whose reward is
its weapon.

## The weapons

### Warrior

**Brakk, Total Fracture** (two-handed sword)
- **Brakk's Frenzy**: your attacks can carry you into a frenzy that grows with
  each stack: more damage, faster attacks, and a larger size.

**Drakthor, the Scalebreaker** (one-handed sword, main hand)
- **Drakthor's Compulsion**: your attacks can subdue the enemies around you,
  bosses included: they can neither move nor attack.

**Malthen, Eternal Aegis** (shield)
- **Malthen's Runic Barrier**: damage taken can raise a barrier that reduces
  the damage you take and strikes back at every melee attacker.
- **Malthen's Vigilance**: greatly increases the threat you generate.

**Vorgath, Bane of Dragons** (two-handed sword)
- **Vorgath's Roar**: your attacks can make Vorgath roar, damaging and stunning
  the enemies around you. The damage grows with your attack power.
- **Vorgath's Exaltation**: your attacks can exalt the allies around you: more
  damage, harder critical strikes and faster attacks.

**Draemorr, Worldbreaker** (two-handed sword)
- **Draemorr's Cleave**: your attacks can unleash a storm of blades: you whirl
  again and again, striking the enemies around you with your weapon, without it
  hindering your other actions.
- **Draemorr's Evisceration**: your attacks disembowel the enemies around you,
  who then suffer Shadow damage over time, stacking.

### Paladin

**Ashbringer, Bane of the Dead** (two-handed sword)
- **Ashbringer's Aura**: an aura that makes the raid attack faster.
- **Ashbringer**: increases your attack power against undead.
- **Sword of Justice**: your attacks can bring down a sword of light that
  impales the target for Holy damage.
- **Hand of Justice**: your melee attacks can grant you an extra attack.

**Vindictus, Burning Retribution** (one-handed mace, main hand)
- **Seraph's Shield**: your attacks can hurl holy shields at the nearest
  enemies; each shield bounces from enemy to enemy, dealing Holy damage and
  slowing those it hits.
- **Vindictus's Shield**: attacks taken can strengthen your guard: a higher
  chance to block, and Holy damage to the attacker for every blocked attack.
- **Vindictus's Consecration**: attacks taken consecrate the ground beneath
  your feet.
- **Burning Retribution**: greatly increases the threat you generate.

**Salvation, Aegis of Sanctuary** (shield)
- **Aegis of Sanctuary**: when the group as a whole falls low on health, the
  whole raid becomes invulnerable for a while, and you kneel in place,
  unstoppable. It then needs a long time to recharge.

**Solareth, Breath of Life** (one-handed mace, main hand)
- **Solareth's Breath**: every direct heal releases a second breath: an extra
  heal on the target and a heal on the allies around it, repeated on the
  target of your Beacon of Light.

**Kyresion, Bulwark of the Righteous** (shield)
- **Aura of Penance**: increases the healing received by the allies around you.
  A light bathes your steps (visual only).

### Hunter

**Kael’Thorr, Bloody Howl** (bow)
- **Kael'Thorr's Precision**: your shots can unleash a volley that sweeps every
  enemy in a cone in front of you. Its damage is split between the targets hit
  and ignores armor, resistances and invulnerability.
- **Gatling**: your shots can carry you away: much faster attacks, part of the
  targets' armor ignored and more damage.
- **Needles of Dawn** (use): creates Needles of Dawn, the bow's arrows.

### Rogue

**Sanguineus, the Silent** (dagger)
- **Sanguineus's Shiv**: your melee attacks can deliver a shiv that deals
  damage and awards you a combo point.

**Carnifex, the Pernicious** (dagger)
- **Carnifex's Fang**: your melee attacks can sharpen the fang: faster attacks
  and harder critical strikes, stacking.

**Shuor'ror, Twilight of Knowledge** (one-handed sword)
- **Shuor'ror's Alacrity**: your melee attacks can restore energy.
- **Shuor'ror's Rage**: your melee attacks can enrage you: more damage and
  faster attacks.

**Shuadslayern, Dawn of Oblivion** (one-handed sword)
- **Shuadslayern's Extermination**: your melee attacks can trigger the
  Extermination: more damage and harder critical strikes, but slower attacks.

### Priest

**Sha'haggul, Voice of Torments** (staff)
- **Dark Needles**: your spells can release a burst of Shadow needles at the
  target.
- **Shadow Missile**: your spells can hurl a Shadow Missile that eradicates the
  target's soul with Shadow damage.

**Luminaris, Eternal Blessing** (staff)
- **Presence of Luminaris**: an aura that reduces the damage the raid takes;
  it also wraps the allies around you in Power Word: Shield at regular
  intervals, and Luminaris's wings envelop you.
- **Infusion of Luminaris**: grants Power Infusion to the allies around you at
  regular intervals.
- A light bathes your steps (visual only).

### Death Knight

**Varkhal, Blade of the Frozen Silence** (one-handed sword, main hand)
- **Varkhal's Rest**: your melee attacks can freeze time for a while: each of
  your hits then chills its victim with Frost damage over time and slows its
  attacks, stacking.

**Skorrn, Bite of the Black Winter** (one-handed sword, off hand)
- **Black Winter**: your melee attacks can unleash the Black Winter, which
  strikes the enemies around you with Frost damage again and again.

**Vorthalak, Scourge of the Living** (two-handed axe)
- **Vorthalak's Rune**: your attacks can carve a rune into the ground beneath
  the target; it deals Shadow damage over time to the enemies around it.
- **Vorthalak's Rot**: your Frost Fever can inoculate Necrotic Frost Fever, and
  your Blood Plague Necrotic Plague. Both deal damage over time that grows with
  your attack power, and both stack without limit.

**Drethmâr, Throne of Blood** (two-handed axe)
- **Grip of the Blood Throne**: your damage can trigger the Grip, which keeps
  pulling the enemies around you towards you (Death Grip) and deals Shadow
  damage to them.
- **Blood Throne**: every hit taken strengthens your throne: more armor, more
  resistances and more health, stacking.
- **Drethmâr's Authority**: greatly increases the threat you generate.

### Shaman

**Ezh’ra, Voice of Thunder** (staff)
- **Ezh'ra's Rumble**: your offensive spells unleash a chain lightning that
  jumps from enemy to enemy.
- **Ezh'Rah's Ascension**: your offensive spells can raise you into Ascension:
  while it lasts, Nature damage strikes the enemies around you again and again,
  and tendrils of lightning race across the ground.

**Nairūn, Breath of the Ancients** (one-handed mace)
- **Call of the Ancients**: each direct heal calls upon the Ancients, who
  answer with one gift drawn at random: an extra Healing Wave, a Chain Heal,
  or, more rarely, a protection that makes the raid around you immune to all
  damage. The protection has its own recharge.

**Kelrah, Harmony of the Elements** (shield)
- **Embrace of the Spirits**: in combat, constantly heals the raid around you,
  and regularly dispels a poison and a disease from the allies around you.

**Drokhan, Fist of the Storms** (one-handed mace, main hand)
- **Drokhan's Fury**: your melee attacks can fire up the raid around you:
  faster attacks and faster casting.

**Mak'hor, Strike of the Spirits** (one-handed mace, off hand)
- **Mak'hor's Rage**: your melee attacks set the ground ablaze: Fire damage to
  the enemies around you, then more Fire damage over time in the area.

### Mage

**Vaelthis, Spellbreaker** (staff)
- **Vaelthis's Infusion**: your offensive spells can infuse you: more damage,
  more haste, and a larger size.
- **Vaelthis's Pyrotechnics**: your offensive spells can set off a volley:
  Fire damage to the enemies in front of you, then burning over time,
  stacking.
- **Vaelthis's Spellbreaker**: your offensive spells steal a beneficial magic
  effect from the target.

### Warlock

**Xer’thul, Master of Chains** (staff)
- **Xer'Thul's Corruption**: your offensive spells sow Seed of Corruption on
  the enemies in a cone in front of you.
- **Xer'Thul's Incineration**: your offensive spells can incinerate the
  target, afflicting it with Xer'Thul's Corruption (Shadow damage over time)
  and Xer'Thul's Immolation (Fire damage, then Fire damage over time). Both
  stack.
- **Demonic Swarm**: the ticks of these two afflictions can tear a demon from
  the Void, drawn at random, to fight for you for a while.

### Druid

**Elun'dor, Voice of the Silent Skies** (staff)
- **Celestial Cycle**: in combat, alternates between two phases. Lunar Zenith:
  more damage and harder spell critical strikes, while lunar shards rain down
  in front of you. Solar Zenith: faster casting and faster movement, while
  sunbeams strike in front of you.
- **Elun'Dor's Plenitude**: your offensive spells can open the Plenitude: for a
  while, the allies around you keep gaining stacks of Elun'Dor's Favor, which
  increases their damage.

**Liora, Lullaby of the Roots** (staff)
- **Liora's Sap**: each tick of your heals over time leaves Sap on the ally
  healed, a heal over time that stacks. As the Sap builds up, it also grants
  Liora's Vigor, which increases the healing that ally receives.
- **Call of the Roots**: in combat, when most of your group has fallen, brings
  every fallen member back to life. It then needs a long time to recharge.

**Tharnok, Spine of the World** (polearm)
- **Frost Brambles**: every hit taken grows the brambles, which return Frost
  damage to each attacker, melee or spell, stacking.
- **Impenetrable Hide**: every hit taken hardens your hide: less damage taken,
  more damage dealt and a larger size, stacking.
- **Spine of the World**: greatly increases the threat you generate.

**Bloodfang the Flayer** (polearm)
- **Butchery**: every melee attack also cuts down the enemies in front of you.
- **Flay**: against an enemy low on health, your attacks can flay it: damage
  that ignores armor and grows with your attack power, and the enemy then
  takes more damage.

## Installation

### With the installer

Stop the world server and close the game, then run `installer.exe`, the
WoW-mods installer (`installer/` folder of this repository), and give it this
folder, or drop the folder on `installer.exe`. Keep the package where you
downloaded it: the installer refuses to run from your server's `modules`
folder. Its window asks for the world server folder and the game folder, finds
the rest (the sources, the database, `mysql.exe`), shows what it found of the
module, and offers **Install** when there is no trace of it.

Installing puts in place:

- the sources, into `modules/mod-artifact-weapons` of your AzerothCore sources;
- the module's rows of seven DBC files (`Item`, `ItemDisplayInfo`, `Spell`,
  `SpellVisual`, `SpellVisualEffectName`, `SpellVisualKit`,
  `SpellVisualKitModelAttach`), added to the server's `Data/dbc` files and
  merged into the game's own files inside its archives;
- the game files of `data/client` and `data/client-surcharges` (models,
  textures, icons, spell effects): into the last custom archive the game reads,
  or into a new `Data\patch-Z.MPQ` when there is none.

The module's identifiers sit in tranche 84 of the repository's register,
`ID_RANGES.md`. When a server already uses one of them for something else, the
installer lists each one and installs nothing.

Then **rebuild the core**, the world server stopped: the installer prints the
commands. On first start, the core updater applies `data/sql/db-world/base` to
the world database.

### By hand

1. **Sources.** Copy this folder into the `modules/` folder of your
   AzerothCore sources, as `modules/mod-artifact-weapons`. The `data/client`,
   `data/client-surcharges` and `data/dbc` folders are not needed there. Run
   the CMake configuration again, then build `worldserver`.

2. **Database.** On first start, the core updater applies the files of
   `data/sql/db-world/base` to the world database, as long as
   `Updates.EnableDatabases` in `worldserver.conf` includes it. Otherwise,
   apply them yourself, in the order of their numbers:

   ```
   mysql -u <user> -p <world database> < data/sql/db-world/base/artifact_weapons_01_item_template.sql
   ```

3. **Server DBC.** Each file of `data/dbc/serveur` holds only the module's
   rows. With a DBC editor, add them to the file of the same name in the
   `Data/dbc` folder of the world server.

4. **Client DBC.** Take the same seven files from the game
   (`DBFilesClient\<name>.dbc`), each from the last archive that contains it,
   so that the rows of your other patches are kept. Add to each the rows of the
   file of the same name in `data/dbc/client`.

5. **Client archive.** With an MPQ editor, create `Data\patch-Z.MPQ` in the
   game folder (or open the last custom archive the game reads), then add:
   - the seven DBC files of step 4, under `DBFilesClient\`;
   - the content of `data/client` and of `data/client-surcharges`, keeping
     their folder paths (`Interface\Icons\...`, `Item\ObjectComponents\...`,
     `Spells\...`).
